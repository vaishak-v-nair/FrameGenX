## I-HallA evaluation bundle

`data/evaluation/ihalla_v1_prefix/manifest.json` records 49 hashed files totaling
14,093,865 bytes: five spreadsheets, four public-folder listings and 40 images.
The prefix contains images 1-10 for history/science and each normal/weird variant
under the upstream DALL-E-3 folders. It is not the full I-HallA benchmark.

Domain-correct workbook parsing found 100 nonempty captions and 500 QA questions
in each domain. The selected images have 200 question instances; normal/weird
variants share questions, so this is 20 domain/index case families, not 200
independent cases. Public source answers and normal/weird labels remain
unreviewed. Image rights are separate from the repository's MIT code license.

No I-HallA image is a positive diffusion target. No VQA provider was called and
no factual-accuracy score was produced. The two Windows acquisition failures
and their logs are retained; the successful retry verified every planned image.
Repeating acquisition rechecks recorded hashes without silently redownloading or
accepting changed files.

## VSM

The previously acquired pinned Cards archive and all 40 templates are reused.

the expanded SDXL adapter study does not replace or qualify that result. Chess
was not added: it does not directly address this corpus's count/color/spatial
targets and would add a large unrelated download.

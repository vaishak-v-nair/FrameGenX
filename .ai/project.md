# FrameGenX

## Mission
To transition generative visual AI from a probabilistic art form into a deterministic engineering discipline by explicitly decoupling intent representation, spatial planning, visual generation, and constraint verification.

## Problem
Current monolithic text-to-image architectures operate as probabilistic associative engines. They lack an intrinsic understanding of physics, object permanence, and strict spatial logic. They produce images that look right but fail structurally upon inspection, causing massive losses in time and capital for automated workflows that require predictability.

## Users
- Systems Engineers
- Synthetic Data Scientists
- Autonomous Agents (LLMs needing a verifiable visual cortex)

## Market
The market is saturated with platforms offering "better aesthetics." There is a critical, unmet need for **controllability, consistency, and verifiability**. FrameGenX targets the enterprise automation and synthetic data markets where a 20% hallucination rate is unacceptable.

## Value Proposition
FrameGenX transforms visual generation from an unpredictable art into a verifiable, deterministic engineering process. You describe the logic via a strict contract, and the system mathematically proves the resulting image satisfies that logic.

## Long-Term Vision
We aim to build the foundational open-source framework for "Agentic Image Generation." Visual generation isn't a final output reviewed by a human; it is a verifiable programmatic step within larger autonomous systems—evolving into video generation, algorithmic storyboarding, and enterprise advertising pipelines.

## Research Questions
1. Does explicit spatial planning and post-generation verification significantly increase constraint satisfaction?
2. Can a visual system autonomously repair its own logical failures?
3. What is the limit of complex numeracy and attribute isolation in 6GB VRAM environments using sequential execution?

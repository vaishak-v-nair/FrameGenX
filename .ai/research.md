# Research Hypothesis

## Primary Hypothesis
Explicit contracts improve constraint satisfaction compared to prompt-only generation.

## Success Criteria
+20% constraint satisfaction rate over baseline models.

## Failure Criteria
No statistically significant improvement in constraint satisfaction.

## Benchmark Dataset
100 meticulously crafted prompts targeting specific failure modes (numeracy, spatial relations).

## Metrics
- Object Accuracy
- Color Accuracy
- Spatial Accuracy
- OCR Accuracy
